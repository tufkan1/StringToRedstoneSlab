### Description
Turns strings (tripwire) into **3D Redstone Slabs** and colors sand **Pitch Black** for high-visibility, error-free cactus farm construction.

- **Supported Versions:** 1.21 – 26.3+
- **Optional:** If you don't want black sand, just delete `sand.png` and `red_sand.png` from `assets/minecraft/textures/block/`.
- **Source Code:** https://github.com/tufkan1/StringToRedstoneSlab
