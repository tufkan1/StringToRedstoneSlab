=====================================================
 String to Redstone Slab (Cactus Farm Visibility Pack)
=====================================================

Author: Tufkan (https://github.com/tufkan1)
Repository: https://github.com/tufkan1/StringToRedstoneSlab

Description:
Replaces tripwire/string textures and models with a redstone slab,
and makes sand/red sand dark black to dramatically improve visibility
and alignment when constructing high-efficiency cactus farms in Minecraft.

Credits & Inspirations:
- Developed and packaged by Tufkan (tufkan1)
- Model logic inspired by Redstone Tweaks (RexxStone) and Respackopts (KikuGie)
- Tested and optimized for Minecraft Java Edition 1.21 through 26.x+
